Permissions and access

Who can do what in the app, and which Jira permissions the app itself asks for.

Who can do what#

The app has no roles of its own. Anyone who can open the app can see and change every record in it: there is no private lead, deal or contact. Treat access to the app as access to your whole customer list.

ActionAny user of the appJira administrator
View records, reports and the audit trailYesYes
Create, edit, delete and merge recordsYesYes
Import, export, bulk actionsYesYes
Save your own viewsYesYes
Change Settings (currency, stale threshold)NoYes

Owner and assignee are labels that say who is responsible. They do not restrict who can edit the record.

Who can open the app at all is controlled by Jira: the Apps menu entry is available to people who can use Jira on the site.

What the app asks of Jira#

Installing the app asks a Jira administrator to approve three permissions:

PermissionWhat it is used for
storage:appThe app's own storage, where your records are kept.
read:jira-workChecking that a work item key you type exists and showing its summary and status.
read:jira-userShowing people's names and searching for people in the owner and assignee pickers.

The app has no permission to create, edit or comment on anything in Jira. Every Jira lookup is made as the signed-in user, so it sees only what that person can see.
