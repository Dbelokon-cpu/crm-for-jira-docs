Dashboard

The numbers grouped by what you would do about them, and tiles that take you straight to the records behind them.

What is on it#

The Dashboard answers "what needs me today?" before "how are we doing?". Its sections:

SectionWhat it shows
Needs attentionCounts of things to act on: overdue tasks, overdue next steps, deals past their close date, stale deals, stale leads and potential duplicates.
TotalsHow many leads, contacts, companies and deals you have.
PipelineOpen deals, open pipeline value, weighted pipeline and average deal.
PerformanceFor a chosen period (7, 30 or 90 days, or all time), and optionally one assignee: won value, deals won and lost, win rate, new leads, average deal and sales cycle. With a period chosen, each tile shows the change against the period before it.
Pipeline by stageHow many deals and how much value sit in each stage.
Deals Closing Soon, Tasks Due SoonThe next things on the calendar.
Recently Won, Recent ActivityWhat just happened.
TrendsNew leads, revenue and win rate over the last few months.

Tiles are shortcuts#

A tile that names a group of records is a filter. Click Stale or No Next Step and you land on the Deals list already narrowed to exactly those deals. Use it as a daily starting point.

How the key numbers are worked out#

  • Win rate: deals won divided by deals won plus lost in the period. Open deals are not counted. With no closed deals it shows a dash rather than 0%.
  • Sales cycle: the average time from creating a deal to winning it, for deals won in the period.
  • Weighted pipeline: see Leads, deals and the pipeline.

The currency of every amount comes from Settings.
