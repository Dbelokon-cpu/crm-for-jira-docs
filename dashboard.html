Dashboard

Where sales stand at a glance: headline figures against the period before, the pipeline funnel, revenue by month, and the deals and tasks that come next.

What is on it#

Open Dashboard. From top to bottom:

PartWhat it shows
Headline figuresEight cards: open deals, pipeline value, weighted pipeline, revenue won, deals won, win rate, new leads and conversion rate.
Pipeline funnelHow many deals sit in each stage, each stage in its own colour.
Revenue trendThe value of deals won in each of the last six months.
Active dealsThe open deals closing soonest, with amount and stage. A close date that has passed is red.
My open tasksYour own open tasks, soonest first, with priority.

Period and assignee#

Choose 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or All time at the top, and optionally one assignee. Revenue won, deals won, win rate, new leads and conversion rate follow the period. Open deals and pipeline value are as they stand now.

The change beside a figure#

With a period chosen, a figure shows how it moved against the stretch of the same length just before: green when it moved the good way, red the bad way, yellow when it stayed the same. For lost deals, going up is the bad way. Hover the change to see the earlier value. All time has nothing before it, so it shows no change.

View all#

View all beside Active deals opens the Deals table with only open deals, sorted by close date. Beside My open tasks it opens Tasks with only your open tasks.

How the key numbers are worked out#

  • Win rate: deals won divided by deals won plus lost in the period. Open deals are not counted.
  • Conversion rate: of the leads created in the period, the share converted to a deal.
  • Weighted pipeline: see Leads, deals and the pipeline.

The currency of every amount comes from Settings. Things to act on today (overdue, stale, no next step) are counted at the top of the Leads, Deals and Tasks pages, where a click filters the list to them.

How fresh the figures are#

Dashboard and Reports figures are worked out from a summary the app refreshes every few minutes, so a change you just saved can take a little while to show. The line under the title says when the figures were worked out; press Refresh to update them now.
