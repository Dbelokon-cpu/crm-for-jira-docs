Add a lead

Open Leads and press Create Lead. Only the name is required. The rest helps you decide whom to call first:

FieldWhat it is for
StatusNew, Contacted, Qualified, Converted or Lost.
RatingHot, Warm or Cold: how likely the lead is to buy.
SourceWhere it came from: Web, Referral, Advertisement, Trade Show, Cold Call, Partner or Other. Reports use it to show which sources convert.
AssigneeWho is responsible. Leads with no assignee can be found with the Unassigned filter.
Contact and companyLink to an existing contact or company, or just type the names.
Email, phoneChecked for a sensible format. Two leads (or contacts) cannot share one email address.
Jira work itemOptional. See Linking to Jira.

A lead can also carry products, the things the prospect asked about. They carry over when you convert.

Board and table

Switch between Table and Kanban with the buttons above the list. The board has a column per status. The table has sortable columns (click a heading) and the same quick filters as everywhere: see Search, filters and saved views. The tiles above the list, such as My Leads and Unassigned, are filters too.

Converting a lead

Open the lead and press Convert to Deal, then confirm. The app:

  • creates a deal in the New stage, with an amount equal to the lead's products;
  • uses the lead's contact and company if they are linked, or finds a company with the same name, or creates a new contact and company from the names on the lead;
  • carries over the lead's products;
  • marks the lead Converted and links the two records both ways.

The lead's card then shows the Converted deal, and you can open it from there.

Losing a lead

Set the status to Lost and choose a reason: Unqualified, No Response, Not Interested, Duplicate, Wrong Contact or Other. Reports count lost leads by reason, so a vague "Other" is a missed chance to learn something.
