Reports and the period

Open Reports. The page is a set of cards in sections. Pick a period at the top (This month, Last month, This quarter, This year or Last 30 days) to see the figures that depend on time.

Compare with the previous period

The comparison card sets the chosen period against the one before it: won value, deals won, deals lost, win rate and deals created, with the change in each. For win rate the change is in percentage points. It can be exported to CSV.

Report snapshot

Press Report snapshot for a short text summary of the period. Select it all (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A), copy it and paste it into a Jira comment, a Confluence page or a chat message; they all display it formatted. It is a good way to put the week's numbers in front of people who do not open the app.

Pipeline

  • Deals by Stage and Pipeline Value by Stage
  • Forecast by Close Month: expected value by the deals' close dates
  • Open Pipeline by Owner
  • Average Days per Stage: where deals wait
  • Deals by Type

Leads

  • Leads by Status, Source and Rating
  • Conversion Rate by Source and by Rating: which sources and ratings really turn into deals
  • Lost Leads by Reason

Revenue

  • Revenue by Lead Source, by Product and by Category: what won deals brought in
  • Sales Performance by Assignee, with CSV export

Deal outcomes

Won Deals by Reason and Lost Deals by Reason. These are only as good as the reasons people choose when they close a deal.

Accounts, products and operations

  • Companies by Industry and by Size
  • Products by Category and Deal Contacts by Product (who bought what)
  • Tasks by Type, by Priority and Task Load by Assignee

Data quality

Potential Duplicates lists records that probably describe the same thing: companies with the same name or website, contacts with the same email or phone, products with the same SKU. Open each and merge where it is a real duplicate.

Reports are calculated from the records in the app at the moment you open them, so they are always current. They can be a little slow on a site with a lot of data: see Limits and behaviour.
