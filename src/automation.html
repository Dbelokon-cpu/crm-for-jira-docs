The four actions

Search for "Dealtrail CRM" when adding an action to a rule.

ActionWhat it does
Move deals in Dealtrail CRMMoves the open deals linked to a work item to a stage. Won and lost deals stay as they are, and a work item with no deals is skipped without an error.
Create lead in Dealtrail CRMCreates a lead, linked to the work item if you keep {{issue.key}}.
Add note in Dealtrail CRMAdds a note to every linked record of the type you choose.
Create task in Dealtrail CRMCreates a task on the work item's linked record, taking a deal first, then a contact, company or lead.

Text fields accept smart values such as {{issue.summary}}.

Example

When a work item moves to Done, move its deals to Won.

The first time you add a step

Jira asks you to connect Automation to the app. Rule runs then act as the person on that connection, and the record's history shows their name.

When a step fails

The rule stops, and the reason is in the rule's audit log.

What rules cannot do

A rule can't start from a change in the CRM: Jira doesn't let apps add their own triggers.
