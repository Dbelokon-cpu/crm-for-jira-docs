How much data is comfortable

The app loads your records when it opens and does the searching, sorting and filtering in your browser. That makes it quick and simple for a small team. It also means that the more records you have, the longer it takes to open, and the longer reports take to calculate.

A few thousand records per section is comfortable. If the app starts to feel slow to open or to save, tell your administrator and contact support. Pages that need to look at everything, such as global search, Potential Duplicates and some of the revenue reports, feel it first.
