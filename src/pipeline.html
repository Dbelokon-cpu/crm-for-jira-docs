From lead to deal

A lead is someone who has shown interest but is not yet a customer. A deal is a sale you are actively working. The two are separate records on purpose: most leads never become deals, and you do not want them in your forecast.

A lead moves through five statuses: New, Contacted, Qualified, Converted or Lost. When a lead is qualified, you convert it. That creates the deal and links the two, so you can always see where a deal came from.

The six stages

There is one pipeline, with six stages. Each has a default chance of winning:

StageWin probabilityMeaning
New10%Just created, not yet worked
Discovery25%You are learning what the customer needs
Negotiation50%Terms are being discussed
Proposal75%A proposal is with the customer
Won100%Closed, the customer signed
Lost0%Closed, no sale

One pipeline is enough for most small teams. The stages are fixed: you cannot add or rename them.

Weighted pipeline

A raw pipeline total treats a brand new deal and a nearly signed one as equal. Weighted pipeline multiplies each open deal's amount by its probability, so it is a more realistic forecast.

Example

If you know a particular deal is more or less likely than its stage says, set a probability on that deal. It replaces the stage's default for that deal only.

Won and lost, and why

Moving a deal to Won or Lost asks for a reason. The reasons are what the Deal outcomes report counts, so they turn a hunch ("we keep losing on price") into a number.

  • Won: Best Price, Product Fit, Relationship, Switched from Competitor, Referral, Other.
  • Lost: Price, Lost to Competitor, No Budget, No Decision Made, Bad Timing, Other.

Stale deals

A deal is stale when it has not changed stage for a number of days. The default is 14; a Jira administrator can change it in Settings. Stale deals are flagged in the board and the table, and there is a Stale filter for them. A deal that is Won or Lost is never stale.

The same threshold applies to leads: a lead whose status has not changed for that long, and which is not yet Converted or Lost, is flagged stale.
