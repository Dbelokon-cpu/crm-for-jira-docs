Contacts

A contact is a person. Open Contacts and press the create button. Only the name is required.

FieldNotes
TitleTheir job title.
Email, secondary email, phoneChecked for a sensible format. Two contacts cannot share one email address.
LinkedInA link to their profile.
CompanyThe company they work for.
Reports toAnother contact, so you can see who works for whom.
TagsComma-separated, for example partner, conference.
Do not contactTick it for people who must not be contacted. A quick filter shows or hides them.
OwnerWho looks after the relationship.

Companies

A company is an organisation. Fields: website, industry, address (city, state, ZIP, country), employees, annual revenue, LinkedIn, tags, owner and a Parent company so you can model subsidiaries.

A company's card lists the contacts who work there and the deals attached to it. Reports group companies by industry and by size.

Merging duplicates

If the same person or company has been entered twice:

  1. Open the duplicate you want to get rid of.
  2. Choose Merge into and pick the record to keep.
  3. Press Merge and Delete.

Everything that pointed at the duplicate (deals, leads, tasks, notes and, for companies, contacts) now points at the record you kept. Merging cannot be undone. To find candidates, open Reports → Potential Duplicates.

Deleting

Deleting a contact or company that has linked deals, tasks or contacts shows a warning that lists what will be affected, so nothing disappears by accident.
