The example

These docs follow one small sale so you can trace it from page to page. You do not need the same data.

Example: Northwind Traders

A company called Northwind Traders asks about an annual licence. Sarah Miller is the person you talk to. You expect to sell the Team licence product for 12,000 USD and hope to close by the end of the month.

Add a lead

  1. Open CRM for Jira from the Jira Apps menu and choose Leads.
  2. Press Create Lead. Enter the name Northwind Traders: annual licence, contact Sarah Miller, her email, the source Referral and the rating Warm. Choose yourself as assignee.
  3. Press Create Lead to save. Only the name is required.

Convert it to a deal

  1. After a call, set the lead's status to Contacted, then Qualified.
  2. Open the lead and press Convert to Deal. Confirm.
  3. The lead is marked Converted and shows the new deal under Converted deal. Open Deals to see it in the New column.

Converting asks you to confirm because it creates several records at once: the deal, and a contact and company if none matched. The lead's products carry over; the lead is kept for your history.

Work the deal

  1. Open the deal. Add the product Team licence (create it first under Products with a price of 12,000). The deal's amount follows its products.
  2. Set a Close date and a Next step, for example Send proposal with a due date.
  3. Move the deal to Discovery, then Proposal, using the stage selector. Add a note after each call.
  4. Add a task from the card: Call Sarah on Friday.

Close it

  1. When the customer signs, press Mark as Won and choose a reason, for example Product Fit.
  2. Open Dashboard: the deal now counts in Deals Won and in the win rate.
  3. Open Reports to see it under Won Deals by Reason and in the period comparison.

If you lose a deal instead, press Mark as Lost and pick a reason. Always pick one: reports break results down by reason.
