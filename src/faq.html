Do I need a separate login?
No. You use your Jira account. Open the app from the Apps menu.
Does the app change anything in Jira?
No. It only reads: a work item's summary and status when you link one, and people's names. It never creates, edits or comments on a Jira issue.
Can I see only my own records?
You can filter to yours with My Leads, My Deals and the like, but the app has no per-record privacy. Everyone who can open it sees everything. See Permissions and access.
Can I add my own stages or have more than one pipeline?
No. There is one pipeline with six fixed stages. See Leads, deals and the pipeline.
Can I drag deals between columns?
No. Use the stage selector on the card or in the table row.
Can I send email or log meetings from the app?
No. The app does not connect to email or a calendar and does not store attachments. Write what happened as a note, and add a task for what comes next.
Can I move my data in from a spreadsheet?
Yes, with CSV import for contacts, companies, leads, deals, products and tasks. See Import from CSV.
Can I get my data out?
Yes. Export CSV from any list, or everything from Settings. See Bulk actions, import and export.
Does changing the currency convert my amounts?
No. It only changes the symbol. There is one currency for the whole workspace.
What happens if two people edit the same record?
The last save wins. The timeline shows who changed what, so you can see it happened.
Can I undo a delete or a merge?
No. Both are permanent. The audit trail shows what was removed.
How many records can it hold?
A few thousand per section is comfortable. See Limits and behaviour.
Where is the data stored?
In Atlassian's storage for the app, on your own Atlassian site. See the privacy policy.
