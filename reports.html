Reports

Seven ready-made reports, each a table with a Total row, a period where it has one, and CSV export.

The list of reports#

Open Reports to see the reports by name and description. Click a name to open it on a page of its own; ← Reports takes you back to the list.

ReportWhat it showsPeriod
Sales Performance by AssigneeFor each assignee: deals in total, open, won and lost; total, won and lost amounts; win rate; and their share of the revenue won.No
Sales by PeriodDeals won in the period: when, amount, company, contact and owner.Yes
Open Deals by PeriodDeals still open that were created in the period: stage, amount, close date, company and owner.Yes
Lost Deals by PeriodDeals lost in the period, with the loss reason.Yes
Pipeline by StageOpen deals grouped by stage, with a subtotal for each stage: amount, amount weighted by win probability, probability, close date and owner.No
Forecast by Close MonthOpen deals by the month they are expected to close, with deals whose close date has passed and deals with no close date on rows of their own.No
Leads by SourceLeads created in the period by source: open, converted, lost and the conversion rate.Yes

On a report's page#

  • Under the title: how many entries the report has, and the total amount where it has one.
  • A period, on the reports that use one: This month, Last month, This quarter, This year, Last 30 days or All time.
  • The last row, Total, adds up counts and amounts; rates in it are worked out from those totals, not added.
  • Export CSV gives the table as text to paste into a spreadsheet or save as a .csv file.

Reports that list deals by name read the full list of deals when you open them. The others use the same summary as the Dashboard, so a change you just saved can take a few minutes to show there. See how fresh the figures are.
