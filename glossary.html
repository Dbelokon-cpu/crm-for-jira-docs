Glossary

The terms used across these docs and in the app, in one place.

Amount
The value of a deal, in the workspace currency. Follows the deal's products once any are attached.
Assignee
The person responsible for a lead or a task.
Audit trail
The log of who changed what and when. Also called Activities. The latest 500 entries are kept.
Card
The panel that opens when you click a record's name, with its details, tasks, notes and timeline.
Close date
When you expect a deal to close. Drives the forecast by month and the overdue flag.
Company
An organisation you sell to. Can have a parent company.
Contact
A person at a company, or on their own.
Convert
Turning a qualified lead into a deal. Creates a contact and company if none match.
Deal
A sale you are working on, moving through the pipeline.
Deal type
New Business, Existing Business or Renewal.
Kanban
The board view, with a column per stage (deals) or status (leads).
Lead
A prospect who has shown interest but is not yet a customer.
Line item
A product attached to a lead or deal. Keeps the price it had when added.
Next step
What happens next on a deal, with an optional due date.
Owner
The person responsible for a deal, contact or company.
Pipeline
All your open deals, by stage. The total value of open deals is the open pipeline.
Win probability
The chance a deal will be won. Each stage has a default; a deal can override it.
Rating
Hot, Warm or Cold: how likely a lead is to buy.
Saved view
A named set of filters on one list, kept for you alone.
Sales cycle
The average days from creating a deal to winning it.
SKU
Your own code for a product.
Stage
Where a deal is: New, Discovery, Negotiation, Proposal, Won or Lost.
Stale
A deal or lead that has not moved for longer than the stale threshold.
Timeline
A record's own history, on its card.
Weighted pipeline
Each open deal's amount multiplied by its win probability, added up.
Win rate
Deals won divided by deals won plus lost.
Work item
Jira's name for an issue. A record can store one work item key.
