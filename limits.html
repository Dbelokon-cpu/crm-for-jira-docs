Limits and behaviour

Every limit and fixed behaviour in one place: sizes, formats and what happens at the edges.

Limits#

WhatLimitWhat happens at the limit
Length of a text field (name, title, address and so on)255Anything longer is cut off when saved.
Length of a note2,000Cut off when saved.
Length of a saved view's name60Refused.
Stale threshold1–365 daysRefused outside the range.
Entries kept in the audit trail500The oldest are removed as new ones arrive. See Activities.
Activities shown on screen100The newest 100 of those kept.
Records per section5,000What sections are designed for. Beyond it, opening a section gets slower. See below.

Rows shown per page in a list50Page numbers appear under the table. Search and filters cover the whole list.
Cards shown per Kanban column25The column says how many more there are.

How much data is comfortable#

Each section loads its own records when you open it, and does the searching, sorting and filtering in your browser. Dashboard and Reports use a compact summary instead of reading every record.

Sections are designed for up to 5,000 records each. Settings → Data size shows how close a site is. If a section starts to feel slow, tell your administrator and contact support.

Behaviour#

TopicHow it behaves
CurrencyOne currency for the whole workspace, set in Settings. Amounts are never converted.
PipelineOne pipeline per site. A Jira administrator can change the stages before Won and Lost.
DatesTyped as YYYY-MM-DD. There is no date picker.
Phone numbers7 to 20 characters: digits, spaces, + - ( ) .
Email addressesMust look like an email. Two contacts, or two leads, cannot share one.
Amounts and pricesZero or more.
Kanban boardsDrag a card to change a deal's stage or a lead's status. Won, Lost and a lost lead ask for a reason first.
Dashboard and ReportsWorked out from a summary refreshed every few minutes, so they can trail the latest saves.
SKUsTwo products cannot share one.
Not includedEmail and calendar sync, file attachments on records, email notifications and more than one pipeline.
Export and importCSV as text you copy and paste, not as a file download.
Saved viewsBelong to the person who made them.
DeletingPermanent. There is no recycle bin.
Jira dataThe only thing the app writes is its own read-only fields on linked work items. See Privacy.
