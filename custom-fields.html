Custom fields

Add fields of your own to contacts, companies, deals and leads: text, number, date or dropdown.

Add a field#

Custom fields are set up by a Jira administrator in Settings, and apply to everyone on the site.

  1. In Custom Fields, pick the record type: contact, company, deal or lead.
  2. Press Add Field, name it and choose its type.
  3. Press Save Fields.
TypeNotes
TextUp to 500 characters.
NumberAny number.
DateTyped as YYYY-MM-DD.
DropdownList the options, separated by commas. Up to 30 options.

Each record type can have up to 15 custom fields, and a field's name can be up to 40 characters.

Where they appear#

  • At the bottom of the record's form.
  • In a More fields box on its card.
  • As columns in its CSV export.

Changing a field later#

You can rename a field, reorder it and edit a dropdown's options. Its type is fixed once it is added.

Removing a field hides its values, and they are cleared from a record the next time it is saved.
