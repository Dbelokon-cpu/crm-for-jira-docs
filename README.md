# CRM for Jira

A lightweight CRM that lives inside Jira. Track leads, contacts, companies,
deals, products and tasks in one place, and link any record to a Jira work
item. No outside service and no separate login: you use your Jira account.

Open it from the **Apps** menu in Jira, then choose **CRM for Jira**.

## What you can do

- Capture **leads** and convert them into **deals** in one click
- Move deals through a pipeline and see a weighted forecast
- Keep **contacts** and **companies** together with their notes, tasks and history
- Plan work with **tasks**, due dates and priorities
- Find things fast with search, filters and saved views
- Import and export CSV
- Follow the numbers on the **Dashboard** and in **Reports**
- Link records to Jira work items and share a record by its web address
