Tasks

To-dos, calls, emails and meetings, with due dates and priorities, on their own list or on any record.

Add a task#

Open Tasks and press Create Task, or add one from the Tasks tab of any record. Fields:

FieldNotes
TitleRequired.
TypeTo-do, Call, Email, Meeting or Other.
PriorityHigh, Normal or Low.
DueA date as YYYY-MM-DD.
AssigneeWho has to do it.
Related toA lead, contact, company or deal.
NotesAnything else worth knowing.

Working your list#

A task is open until you complete it. The filters keep your day under control:

  • My Tasks: only those assigned to you.
  • Overdue: past their due date and still open.
  • Due Today.
  • By priority and by type.

The side menu shows a number next to Tasks when something needs attention, and the Dashboard lists tasks due soon.

Select several tasks to change their priority or assignee at once: see Bulk actions.

Deleting#

You are asked to confirm before a task is deleted from a record's card.
