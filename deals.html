Deals

Create deals, move them through the pipeline on a board or in a table, attach products, and close them as won or lost.

Add a deal#

Open Deals and press Create Deal, or convert a lead. Fields:

FieldWhat it is for
NameRequired.
AmountZero or more, in the currency set in Settings. Once you attach products, the amount follows them.
StageOne of the six stages.
Close dateWhen you expect to close. Used by the forecast by month. A deal past its close date is flagged overdue.
Next step and its due dateWhat happens next. Deals with no next step can be found with the No Next Step filter.
TypeNew Business, Existing Business or Renewal.
Win probabilityOptional. Replaces the stage's default for this deal.
Contact, companyWho you are selling to.
OwnerWho is responsible for the deal.
Jira work itemOptional. See Linking to Jira.

Dates are typed as YYYY-MM-DD, for example 2026-11-30. The app has no date picker.

Board and table#

Deals open as a Kanban board: one column per stage, with the number of deals and their total value at the top of each. Press Table view for a sortable list and Kanban view to go back. Stale deals carry a badge showing how many days they have sat in their stage.

Cards cannot be dragged between columns. Change the stage with the stage selector on the card or in the table row.

Products on a deal#

Open a deal and add products from your catalogue as line items. The deal's amount is kept in step with the line items, so you do not have to add them up. A line item keeps the price it had when you added it, so changing the catalogue price later does not rewrite existing deals.

Winning and losing#

  • Press Mark as Won and choose a reason. The stage becomes Won.
  • Press Mark as Lost and choose a reason. The stage becomes Lost.

The reasons feed Deal outcomes. Won deals count in the Dashboard's Deals Won and in the win rate.

Deleting a deal#

You are asked to confirm. Deleting removes the deal's line items and notes with it. The deal's history stays in Activities until it ages out.
