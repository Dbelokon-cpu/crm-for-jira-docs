Linking to Jira and sharing records

Point a record at the Jira work item that delivers it, and send a colleague straight to a record.

Linking a record to a work item#

Leads, contacts, companies and deals have a Jira work item field. Type a key such as PROJ-123 and save.

The app checks the item as you: it must exist and you must be allowed to see it. If it does, the record then shows the item's summary and status next to the key, so a deal can point at the ticket that delivers it. The key is looked up at the moment you save; a later change to the item's summary or status is not pulled in until you save the record again.

  • Keys are not case sensitive: proj-123 is stored as PROJ-123.
  • If the key does not exist, or you cannot see it, the save is refused with a message.
  • The app only reads the work item. It never changes anything in Jira.

Sharing records#

Every record card has its own web address. When you open a card, the address in your browser changes to point at it, and changes back when you close it. Copy it from the address bar and paste it into a Jira comment, an email or a chat message; whoever opens it (and has access to the app) lands directly on the same record.

The address carries the record's id in a parameter named for its section: selectedDealId, selectedLeadId, selectedContactId, selectedCompanyId, selectedProductId or selectedTaskId.

If you open a link to a record that has since been deleted, the app opens normally and there is no card to show.
