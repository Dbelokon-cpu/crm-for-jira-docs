Products

A catalogue of what you sell, with price, cost and margin, ready to attach to leads and deals.

The catalogue#

Open Products and create a product for each thing you sell. Fields:

FieldNotes
NameRequired.
SKUYour own code for the product. Two products with the same SKU show up under Potential Duplicates.
PriceWhat you charge. Zero or more.
CostWhat it costs you. The list shows the margin from price and cost.
CategoryFree text, used to group products in reports.
UnitLicense, Service, Project, Subscription or One-time.
DescriptionFree text.

Using products#

Attach products to a lead or a deal as line items. A product's own card shows which deals use it. The Unused filter lists products no deal uses yet, which is handy for tidying the catalogue.

Revenue reports group won deals by product and by category: see Reports → Revenue.

Deleting a product#

You are asked to confirm. Products can be deleted in bulk: see Bulk actions.
