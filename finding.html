Search, filters and saved views

Find a record in seconds, narrow a list to what matters today, and keep your favourite filters one click away.

Search#

Each list has its own search box above it, which searches that section by name and other key fields.

Global search#

The box at the top of the app searches every section at once, including the text of notes. Type at least two characters. Results are grouped by record type with the number of matches in each; click one to open its card.

Filters#

Quick filters sit above each list. Which ones you see depends on the section:

SectionQuick filters
LeadsMy Leads, Unassigned, status, rating, source, stale
DealsMy Deals, Stale, Overdue, No Next Step, Unassigned, stage, type
ContactsMy Contacts, Do Not Contact, Unlinked (no company), company
CompaniesMy Companies, industry, With Open Deals
ProductsUnused, category, unit
TasksMy Tasks, Overdue, Due Today, priority, type

The numbered tiles above a list are filters too. Click a tile and the list narrows to exactly those rows; click it again to clear it. The Dashboard tiles work the same way and open the right list already filtered.

To sort, click a column heading.

Saved views#

When you have filters set, a Save view button appears. Give the view a name (up to 60 characters) and press Save. It shows up as a button above the list: click it to bring those filters back, or press × next to it to delete it.

  • Saved views are yours alone. Colleagues do not see them.
  • A view is a set of filters for one section. It does not freeze the data: the same view next week shows next week's rows.
  • There is nothing to save until a filter is on, so the button is hidden on an unfiltered list.
Example

On Deals, turn on My Deals and Stale, then save the view as My stale deals. Every Monday, one click shows what to chase.
